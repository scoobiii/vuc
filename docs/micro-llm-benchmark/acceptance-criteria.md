# Acceptance Criteria

1. At least one native <=500M model executes through the benchmark runtime, or CI reports BLOCKED/SKIPPED with the exact dependency.
2. No simulated metric can produce PASS.
3. Model tracks are separated.
4. Every PASS has execution evidence.
5. Authorization, sandbox and proof verification are represented.
6. Capacity results are derived from observed processes.
7. Existing VUC gates remain unchanged.
8. Artifacts are published by CI.
